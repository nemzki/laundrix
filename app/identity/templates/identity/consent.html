Identity Verification

Verify Your Identity

Please read this notice before you continue. It takes about two minutes.

Why We Ask

Laundrix checks that the person using an account is who they say they are.

What Happens

  1. You're sent to a secure verification page run by our provider, Didit.
  2. You photograph a government-issued ID. The page checks that it looks genuine and reads it.
  3. You take a live selfie. The page checks that you are a real person present, and compares your face with the photo on your ID.
  4. You come back here and see your result.

What We Collect and Keep

Your ID photos, selfie and face data are handled by the provider on its page. They are not stored on Laundrix servers. Laundrix keeps only: your result (verified, under review, or not verified), simple reason categories, the type of document used (for example "Passport"), and timestamps.

Because the provider processes this data for us, it may be handled outside your country. See the provider's privacy notice for how long it keeps data.

Your Choices and Rights

Using this is your choice. If you'd rather not, or can't, tell your administrator and ask for an alternative.
