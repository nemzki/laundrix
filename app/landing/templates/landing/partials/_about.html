About Laundrix

Built Around the Way Wash Up! Runs Its Day.

Laundrix is a laundry order and customer management system developed for Wash Up! Laundry Shop. It helps organize customer information, laundry services, orders, payments, and daily operations in one place.

Administrators manage services, pricing, and staff accounts, while administrators and staff create orders, record payments, and monitor laundry status. Dashboard overviews and payment summaries help the team review shop activity.
