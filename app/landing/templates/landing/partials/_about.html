Built for Wash Up! Laundry Shop

Laundrix was designed around the everyday work at Wash Up! Laundry Shop. It brings customer records, laundry orders, services, and payments together, so the team can spend less time on paperwork and more time serving customers.

  • For administratorsManage services, pricing, and staff accounts, and review reports in one place.
  • For staffRegister customers, create orders, update their progress, and record payments.
  • Easy to learnClear screens and simple steps keep each task approachable, even for first-time users.
