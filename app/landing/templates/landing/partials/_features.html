Features

Everything You Need to Manage Your Laundry Shop

Six focused tools that cover the everyday work of a laundry shop, without the clutter.

  • Customer Management

    Keep customer names and contact details in one organized record, ready whenever a new order comes in.

  • Laundry Order Processing

    Create laundry orders in a few simple steps and follow each one from drop-off to release.

  • Payment Tracking

    Record payments against each order so staff can quickly see what is paid and what is still due.

  • Services & Pricing

    Set up your laundry services and their prices once, then apply them consistently to every order.

  • QR Order Tracking

    Track orders with QR codes so the right laundry can be found and identified quickly at the counter.

  • Analytics & Reports

    Review summaries of orders and payments to understand how the shop is doing day to day.
