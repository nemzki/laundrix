Privacy notice How we use your ID and face data

You must complete a one-time identity verification with our provider, Didit, using a government ID and a live selfie.

After enrollment, normal staff sign-in uses your work email and password. You will not be asked to repeat the ID and face verification at every login. An administrator may request re-verification when necessary.

Laundrix does not store your ID photos, selfies, or face data. It stores identity-verification records and related audit information.

Didit processes your verification data under its applicable privacy terms. If you have questions or concerns, contact your administrator before proceeding.
